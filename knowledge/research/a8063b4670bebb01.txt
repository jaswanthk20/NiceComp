Refrigerated Air Drier
Copper Coil Refrigerated Air Drier are efficient Reliable and easy solution for clean dry air.
It is dynamically designed to separate water vapor  compressed moisture forming dew point to +3°C.
Features :
Stable Pressure Dew Point :
Typically maintains a 3°C (37°F) dew point, sufficient for over 90% of general industrial applications.
Low Operating Costs :
Extremely energy-efficient compared to desiccant dryers.
Automatic Condensate Drain :
Discharges accumulated water automatically without human intervention or compressed air loss .
Environmentally Friendly Refrigerants :
Modern units use Eco-friendly options.
