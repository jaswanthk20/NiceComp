LC Models:
LC Model are economical low cost Air Compressors designed to suit every pocket. These Air Compressors Starts from 50 Ltrs 1HP to 300 Ltrs 5HP. These are widely used in Painting, Puncture Shops, Engineering Shops, Glass and Wood Industries.

Forged Crank Shaft with roller bearing for smooth, long, continuous running.
Cylinder and Heads auto shell cast with deep finned fins for excess heat absorption.
Dynamic balance heavy compressor pulley with long blades of fan cools the block.
High quality of Piston and Piston Ring helps oil control and smooth oil compressions.
High quality of Piston and Piston Ring helps oil control and smooth oil lubrication.

Continuous R&D for trouble free working and longer life.

PR Models :
PR Models are Premium Air Compressors designed and manufactures
Our Premium model Air Compressors are engineered to deliver superior performance, maximum efficiency, and long-lasting reliability. Built with advanced technology and high-quality components, they ensure consistent compressed Air output while minimizing energy consumption, Designed for demanding industrial applications, these compressors offer low maintenance, quiet operation, and exceptional durability, making them the ideal choice for businesses  seeking high productivity and dependable performance.
Our Premium range of Air Compressors is designed to deliver reliable performance, high efficiency, and exceptional durability for a wide range of industrial applications.
Single Stage Air Compressors :
Compact, Efficient, and Dependable, our Single Stage Compressors are ideal for light to medium duty applications. They Provide consistent air delivery , low maintenance, and economical operation, making them suitable for workshops, automotive service centers, and general industrial use.
Two Stage Air Compressors :
Engineered for high pressure and continuous duty applications, our Two Stage Compressors offer superior air delivery, enhanced efficiency, and extended service life. Built with robust components, they are the preferred choice for manufacturing plants, engineering industries, and other demanding industrial environments where reliable performance is essential.
Multi Stage High Pressure Air Compressors :
Our Multi Stage Air Compressors are specially engineered to produce high pressure compressed air for PET bottle blowing applications. Featuring advanced multi-stage compression, efficient cooling, and durable construction, they deliver clean, reliable, and energy-efficient performance. These Compressors are ideal for PET bottle manufacturing plants, ensuring consistent pressure, high productivity, and dependable operation in continuous production environments.
