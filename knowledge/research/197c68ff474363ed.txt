3. At what frequency we have to change or add the oil
For all the Reciprocating Air Compressors after every 500 hours of rehearing Add oil whenever the oil level is below the mark
For all Rotary Screw Compressors after every 2000 hours rehearing
Note : Industry environment, oil has to be changed frequency after every 200 hours of rehearing
4. What is the importance of oil in compressor?
Oil it provides critical lubrication to reduce friction, absorbs and dissipates extreme heat, creates a tight seal between moving parts, prevents corrosion, and helps clean out tiny debris.
5. What are the disadvantages if we do not maintain oil in Air Compressor
Parts overheat, metal grinds together, the pump seizes, and the motor burns out.
