Air Receivers
An Air Receivers is essentially a heavy-duty pressure vessel that acts as a strong reservoir for compresses air. it helps maintain stable pressure across your facility and prevents your compressor from constantly turning on and off under shifting loads.
Where put the receiver tank before or after the air dryer determines if it holds wet air or dry air.
